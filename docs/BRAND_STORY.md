# Crazy Desert Live Club — The Story

## The Beginning

Crazy Desert Racing started as a dream inspired by the landscapes of the Negev
Desert.

The goal is to create a future community around adventure, racing, friendship,
festivals, and unforgettable experiences under the open sky.

## What the Dream Becomes

Crazy Desert Live Club is more than racing, cars, or festivals.

It is a place for people brought together by the desert, the open road,
freedom, and the desire to create real memories.

Some come for speed. Some for music and nights under the open sky. Others love
travel, camping, photography, or unusual cars.

We are different, but all of us are **Crazy Desert Club Members**.

Here, every road becomes a story. Every meeting becomes a memory. And every
event leaves behind more than photographs — it leaves the feeling:
“I was there. I was part of it.”

**Drive. Explore. Belong.**

Crazy Desert Live Club is not just a place to visit. **It is a place to
belong.**

## Closing Promise

**Today, it is a project.**

**Tomorrow... reality.**

## Member Identity

- Public community label: **Club Member**
- Full profile signature: **Crazy Desert Club Member**
- Interests such as Racing, Festivals, Camping, Music, Photography, and
  Off-Road describe the member; they do not divide the community.
- `USER` and `ADMIN` remain security roles. Club Member is the public identity,
  not an authorization role.
